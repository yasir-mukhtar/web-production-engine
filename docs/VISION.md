# Product Vision

> Session status: structure established in Session 0. Content will be distilled and challenged in Session 1. This draft is not yet authoritative.

## 1. Executive foundation

Define the product in one sentence, its vision, mission, and intended long-term change.

## 2. The problem worth solving

Explain the structural market gap and why faster page generation alone does not solve it.

## 3. Constituencies and obligations

Define obligations to the founder/operator, SMB client, website visitor, and future maintainer.

## 4. Product thesis and positioning

Define the durable category, value proposition, and convictions that make the product coherent.

## 5. Enduring principles

State the decision rules that downstream specifications MUST preserve.

## 6. Values and priority order

Define how truth, outcomes, reliability, accessibility, ownership, repeatability, leverage, and speed rank when they conflict.

## 7. Enduring scope and non-scope

Define the product category and ethical boundaries without freezing a version-one feature list.

## 8. Human and AI philosophy

Define human accountability, appropriate automation, consequential decisions, and prohibited behavior.

## 9. Quality and ownership philosophy

Define quality as demonstrated outcomes and establish ownership, editability, transferability, and recovery as product obligations.

## 10. Business principles

Define value creation, sustainable economics, defensibility, client fit, and boundaries on efficiency claims without choosing pricing or GTM tactics.

## 11. Long-term success and technology evolution

Define durable success and the tests a new model, framework, CMS, host, or interface must pass before adoption.

## 12. Inheritance rules

State how `PRODUCT.md`, `SYSTEM.md`, `QUALITY.md`, pricing, GTM, and operating instructions must derive from this vision.

## Drafting acceptance check

Session 1 is complete when the document is concise, internally consistent, vendor-independent, explicit about trade-offs and exclusions, and strong enough to reject an attractive but misaligned product decision.
